## Field Reference

- **Auth levels:** `public`, `authenticated`, `admin`, `role:[name]`
- **Status progression:** `planned` → `built` (set by whoever builds the page, in the same commit) → `verified` (set in compass STEP 3 by valley-of-death once the page matches the spec, including its auth gate if it has one)
- **API routes do not belong here.** Backend endpoints (the usage counter, auth) stay in PLANNER.md's "API Routes" table. This file is page/UI routes only.
- Legal pages `/privacy` and `/terms` live in a `(legal)` route group and are linked from the footer, not the main nav. There is no `/cookies` (no banner), no `/refunds` (sells nothing), no `/account/delete` (no visitor accounts) and no `/unsubscribe` (no newsletter).
